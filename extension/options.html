Open PDC ↗

Your calendar, your choice

A place for your next seminar.

Sign into PDC as usual. An “Add to calendar” button will appear beside your enrolled events. Choose where to save each event.

Default preferences

Outlook and Google use their own sign-in pages and event editors. No account setup is needed here. Set reminders in their editors.

Connect Nextcloud Optional

Save directly to a calendar you own or can edit. Each person can use their own Nextcloud server.

In Nextcloud Calendar, open the ⋯ menu beside your target calendar and copy its private link. Use the link for that calendar, not a public share or the general Calendar page.

Use a separate app password for PDC Calendar.

Chrome will ask for access only to the Nextcloud server you enter. The app password is stored locally in this extension and is not synced to your Google account or exposed to the PDC page.

Independent student tool, not an official HKUST(GZ) application. No analytics or external service. Calendar events are added only when you choose to add or export them. Later PDC changes are not synchronized.
