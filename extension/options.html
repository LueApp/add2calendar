Your calendar, your choice

A place for campus events and classes.

Sign into PDC or SIS as usual. Add2Calendar appears on supported schedule pages and lets you choose where to save each event.

Default preferences

Outlook and Google use their own sign-in pages and event editors. No account setup is needed here. Set reminders in their editors.

Connect Nextcloud Optional

Save directly to a calendar you own or can edit. Each person can use their own Nextcloud server.

In Nextcloud Calendar, open the ⋯ menu beside your target calendar and copy its private link. Use the link for that calendar, not a public share or the general Calendar page.

Use a separate app password for Add2Calendar.

Chrome will ask for access only to the Nextcloud server you enter. The app password is stored locally in this extension, is not synced to your Google account, and is not exposed to PDC or SIS pages.

Independent student tool, not an official HKUST(GZ) application. No analytics or external service. Calendar entries are created only when you choose to add or export them. Later source changes are not synchronized.
